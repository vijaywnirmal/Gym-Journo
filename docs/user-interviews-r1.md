# Talking to users: Phase 2 discovery

> **Replaced on 6 November 2026** by the self-filled survey (Google Form built by [`discovery-survey.gs`](discovery-survey.gs)). Keep this guide for the follow-up calls with people who agree to try the app; its questions and the "try it" step still apply there.

Before we build Release 1, talk to about eight people. The chats test the hypotheses in
[`product-strategy.md`](product-strategy.md) (H1–H20). The goal is to learn what's true, not to sell
GymJourno. This is a formal gate: the Release 1 stories that depend on these answers don't start
until the first six chats are done (stories needed whatever the answers are are built in parallel).

These are exploratory chats, not a survey: eight people won't represent the Indian fitness market.
They're for finding real problems, testing our guesses and spotting needs we haven't thought of.
Record every chat in the Google Form **GymJourno Phase 2 – Interview notes** (in Vijay's Google
Drive; built by [`discovery-form.gs`](discovery-form.gs)). Answers land in its linked responses sheet;
copy the evidence into the discovery workbook (`GymJourno_Discovery_Workbook.xlsx`) to classify
hypotheses.

## How to run it

- About 20 minutes for individuals, 25 for trainers, 20–25 for a creator-trainer or gym owner. On a
  call or in person; ask if you can take notes.
- You don't need every question. Follow their real experiences and dig into the biggest problems.
- Ask about what they **did**, not what they **would** do. "Last time you…" beats "Would you…?".
- Don't explain GymJourno until the end. Listen more than you talk.
- When they mention a problem, ask "Can you tell me about the last time that happened?" and "What
  did you do about it?" If they did nothing, it may not be a big problem.
- Compliments ("sounds great!") aren't evidence. Time, money or effort they've already spent is.

## Questions

### Individuals (class-goers, lifters, beginners, busy people)

1. Tell me about your last week of exercise. What did you do, and when?
2. Do you keep track of any of it today? How (an app, a notebook, your watch, nothing)?
3. What do you write down, or wish you had? (How long, how hard, which class, how you felt?)
4. Think about your last class or workout. Did you record anything afterwards? If not, what stopped
   you?
5. Is there anything you'd want to look back on after a few months?
6. Do you work towards a goal? What is it, and how do you know you're getting closer?
7. Tell me about the last time you stopped exercising for a while. What happened, and what got you
   back (or didn't)?
8. Have you tried a fitness app and stopped using it? What made you stop?
9. Have you ever had a trainer? How did they keep track of you between sessions?
10. Have you ever paid for a fitness app, training programme or fitness subscription? What did you
    pay for, and why?

### Independent trainers

1. How many clients do you coach right now? Where (gym, homes, online)?
2. Walk me through how you plan a client's week. Where does the plan live?
3. How do you know whether a client actually trained? What do you do when they didn't?
4. Walk me through the last time you followed up with a client who missed a planned workout. What
   did you do, and how much time did it take?
5. How much time a week goes on messages, reminders and chasing?
6. How do you keep track of who's paid?
7. Do you use any app or software for clients now? What do you pay for it? Why did you choose it, or
   why did you stop?
8. If an app did this for you, would your clients use it? What would stop them?
9. At what monthly price would an app like this feel so cheap you'd doubt it? Getting expensive but
   still worth it? Too expensive to consider? Would you rather pay a flat fee or by number of
   clients?

### Creator-trainers

1. How do followers become your clients today? Walk me through the last one.
2. How many do you coach now, and how many more could you handle?
3. Where does the coaching happen (DMs, WhatsApp, Google Forms, an app)? What breaks as it grows?
4. If you could put one link in your Instagram bio for followers who want you to coach them, what
   would they need to see when they tap it? Would you coach them in an app outside Instagram?
5. What do you charge clients, and what do you spend on tools to coach them?
6. What would put you off sending followers to an app?

### Gym owners

1. How many members and trainers do you have? How do members join?
2. How do you know when a member has stopped coming? What do you do about it?
3. How do you track fees and payments today?
4. Do you use any gym software? What do you pay, and what do you wish it did?
5. Would your trainers or members use an app that tracks their workouts? What would stop them?
6. Would you be comfortable with members owning their own workout data, with you seeing activity
   counts only?

### Trainers you already know (the pilot trainer)

1. Do any of your clients do classes, yoga or cardio as well as training with you?
2. How do you hear about that today, if at all?
3. Would you want to see those sessions, and in how much detail?
4. Would you plan sessions like "yoga, Thursday, 45 min" for clients?

### Try it (5 minutes, for at least three of the first six)

Watching someone use the live app beats asking what they think. Ask them to sign up on their own
phone and do one task while you watch, without helping. Note where they hesitate, what they
misunderstand, and what they expected to happen.

| Who | Task on gymjourno.fit (works today) |
| --- | --- |
| Lifter, beginner or busy person | Plan tomorrow's workout, then log one exercise with two sets |
| Class-goer | Find where they'd record today's class (it isn't built yet: watch where they look, then show the mock-up below) |
| Trainer | Create a trainer account, make an invite link, and say who they'd send it to first |
| Trainer (if a client has joined) | Open a client and find what they did this week |

Logging a Zumba session or a cardio workout isn't in the app until Release 1, so for those use the
mock-up below.

### Show and ask (last 5 minutes)

**Individuals:** show this and ask "Would this work for you? What's missing?"

```
Tuesday · Zumba

  Activity   Zumba
  Duration   45 min
  Effort     ●●●●●●●○○○  7/10
  Notes      New routine, loved it

  + Add exercises (optional)
```

**Trainers, creators and gyms:** show the live trainer or gym page (gymjourno.fit/trainers or
gymjourno.fit/gyms) and ask "What here would you actually use? What's missing? Would you try it with
one client (or one trainer) next week?" A "yes" with a date is evidence; "maybe" isn't.

## After each chat

Fill in that person's sheet in the workbook straight away, while it's fresh. Keep three things apart
for every hypothesis it touches:

1. **Evidence:** what they actually said or did (their words, a real example, money or time spent).
2. **Interpretation:** what that suggests.
3. **Decision:** Supported, Rejected or Changed (or "not enough yet").

Example:

| Evidence | Interpretation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gets class schedules on WhatsApp but doesn't record attendance or duration | A simple activity journal may help, but wanting to look back isn't proven | Changed |
